Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

In short

This app does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no ads, analytics or trackers.

What you write

Your agendas, notes, times and reminder choices are stored in your device’s browser storage. They stay on your device unless you turn on account sync or export a backup. Photos you add stay only on your device. They are never uploaded or synced. Your name, if you give one, stays on your device and is used only for the greeting. Quick notes and the contacts you add (names and phone numbers) are stored the same way, only inside the app. The app does not read your phone’s address book. When it spots a phone number in your text, it checks on your device only and asks before saving it. Email addresses, symbols and colors you add to contacts are stored the same way.

Camera and microphone

The app asks for camera or microphone access only when you tap the photo or voice-recording button, and only if you allow it in your browser. Photos you take or pick, and voice recordings you add to notes, are stored only on your device in the browser’s storage. Nothing from the camera or microphone is uploaded, synced or shared, and recordings are not included in backups. You can turn the permissions off at any time in your browser’s site settings.

Backups

When you export a backup, the file is created on your device and saved where you choose. It contains your agendas, notes, contacts and photos at full resolution. You can protect it with a password, in which case it is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) before it is saved. We never receive the file or the password, and a forgotten password cannot be recovered. Keep the file somewhere safe: anyone who has an unencrypted backup can read it. When you import a backup, it is read only on your device.

Sync

When sync is available, your agendas are also saved to the app’s own private cloud storage so they appear on your other devices. Only you can read your own entries there. Notes and contacts are synced the same way.

Fonts

The app’s typefaces are built into the app. Nothing is loaded from other websites, so no third party receives your IP address or browser details.

Reminders

Reminders are created on your device and are not sent to any server. If you allow notifications in your browser’s site permissions, the app uses them only to show a reminder alert and play a sound at the time you set. Nothing is sent anywhere, and you can turn the permission off at any time in your browser’s site settings.

Your control

You can edit or delete any entry, move an agenda to trash and delete it forever, or export a backup. Clearing your browser data removes everything stored locally. Deleting an entry also removes its text from synced storage when sync is on.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated.
